Pigeon mirror self-recognition and Monet/Picasso discrimination; pigeon crop milk made by both parents (flamingos and
emperor penguins too); about 2/3 of octopus neurons in the arms; wild sloths sleeping about 9–10 h; pigeon homing
over 1,000+ km using the sun, smell and magnetic cues; Inky's 2016 escape in New Zealand; sloths holding their breath
for up to about 40 min; octopuses having only one photoreceptor type (so likely colour-blind); sloth digestion taking
weeks; pigeons trained to spot breast-cancer signs in images (2015 study) and to tell letters and faces apart;
veined octopuses carrying coconut shells; three-toed sloths having extra neck vertebrae and turning their heads about 270°.
